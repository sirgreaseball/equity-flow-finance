<p align="center">
  <b>Equity Flow</b> lets anyone own a piece of the local businesses they believe in.<br>
  Small and medium enterprises raise capital by offering fractional equity,<br>
  and investors back them from ₹100, with every share tokenized and on the record.
</p>

<h3 align="center">A marketplace for the businesses next door</h3>

<p align="center">
  Businesses list what they are raising, the equity they offer and how far along they are.<br>
  Investors browse, compare and back them in a few taps.
</p>

<img src=".github/media/marketplace.webp" width="100%" alt="The marketplace of business listings, each with its raise, equity and backers">

<br>
<br>

<h3 align="center">Every listing, an open book</h3>

<p align="center">
  Each business gets its own page: the goal, what has been raised, the equity on offer and who has backed it,<br>
  with a data room, shareholder votes and updates behind it.
</p>

<img src=".github/media/detail.webp" width="100%" alt="A listing page with its funding goal, raise progress and the invest button">

<br>
<br>

<h3 align="center">A way out, not just a way in</h3>

<p align="center">
  A secondary market lets investors sell their stake to other investors,<br>
  so backing a local business no longer means waiting years for an exit.
</p>

<img src=".github/media/trade.webp" width="100%" alt="The secondary market's live order book">
